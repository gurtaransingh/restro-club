# Restro Club Data Model Blueprint

This blueprint defines the first relational boundaries for a PostgreSQL-backed Restro Club platform. The goal is to launch one location while keeping every module multi-location-ready.

## Tenant hierarchy

```text
Organization
└── Location
    ├── Department
    ├── Facility
    │   └── Resource
    └── Staff assignments
```

- `organizations`: owns brand-level settings, billing identity and future expansion controls.
- `locations`: stores address, timezone, operating status and public contact details for each property.
- `departments`: restaurant, kitchen, indoor club, outdoor sports, pool, housekeeping, HR, finance and marketing teams.
- `facilities`: restaurant areas, courts, pool zones, rooms, event lawns and future configurable spaces.
- `resources`: concrete bookable assets such as Table 24, Pickleball Court 1, Pool Session A or Room 2BHK-01.

## Identity and RBAC

- `users`: customer and staff login identity.
- `customer_profiles`: customer preferences, loyalty status and contact fields.
- `employee_profiles`: employee ID, designation, department, manager, joining date and employment status.
- `roles`: Super Admin, General Manager, Restaurant Manager, Chef, Super Chef, Pool Manager, Stay Manager, HR Manager, Finance Manager, Marketing Manager and Customer.
- `permissions`: module/action permissions such as `orders.view`, `orders.refund`, `inventory.manage` and `payroll.export`.
- `role_permissions` and `user_roles`: many-to-many mappings scoped globally or to a location.

## Restaurant and QR ordering

- `restaurant_tables`: table number, area, capacity, status and active QR token.
- `table_sessions`: temporary guest sessions created when a QR code is scanned.
- `menu_categories`, `menu_items`, `menu_item_media`, `menu_item_variants`, `menu_item_addons`: public menu and customization records.
- `recipes`: links menu items to inventory ingredients for food cost and gross margin reporting.
- `orders`, `order_items`, `order_status_events`: immutable ordering flow from received to completed.
- `bills`, `bill_splits`, `payments`: support merged bills, split bills, refunds, taxes and invoices.

## Booking engine

- `booking_types`: restaurant reservation, sports slot, pool access, stay, event and coaching.
- `bookings`: customer, location, facility/resource, date range, capacity, status and payment status.
- `booking_participants`: guest/player lists.
- `availability_rules`: opening hours, slot duration, peak/off-peak pricing and recurrence.
- `maintenance_blocks`: prevents bookings during closures, repairs and private events.

## Operations

- `inventory_items`, `stock_batches`, `suppliers`, `purchase_orders`, `stock_movements`, `waste_records`: purchase-to-consumption tracking.
- `attendance_records`, `leave_requests`, `salary_records`, `payroll_runs`: HR and payroll history without overwriting previous values.
- `notifications`, `notification_templates`, `notification_events`: centralized email, SMS, push and WhatsApp history.
- `audit_logs`: sensitive admin action history with actor, action, entity, before/after JSON, IP, device and session.

## Reporting tables

Analytical projections should eventually be denormalized into summary tables or materialized views:

- `daily_revenue_summary`
- `facility_utilization_summary`
- `menu_item_profitability_summary`
- `customer_lifetime_value_summary`
- `employee_attendance_summary`

These summaries should be generated by background workers so customer requests remain fast.
